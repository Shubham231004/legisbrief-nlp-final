Consumer Data Privacy and Protection Act

This bill requires covered companies to provide consumers with a clear notice describing the categories of personal information they collect, the reasons for collection, and the parties with whom the information is shared. Consumers may request access to their personal information and may ask a company to correct or delete inaccurate records. A covered company must respond within forty-five days and must provide an appeal process when a request is denied. The bill prohibits a company from discriminating against a consumer who exercises these rights. It authorizes the responsible agency to issue regulations, investigate complaints, and impose civil penalties for repeated violations. Small businesses that process limited amounts of information receive additional time to comply, but they must still maintain reasonable data-security safeguards.
